Cookie Policy

Last updated: [insert date this policy is published]

This page explains how this website currently uses cookies and similar technologies.

Current status: no tracking or analytics cookies

As currently built, this website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and it does not use browser local storage to track visitors. It does not require a cookie consent banner at this time because it sets no non-essential cookies.

Files loaded from other services

To display fonts and icons, this site loads resources from Google Fonts and Cloudflare's cdnjs. Loading these files causes your browser to make a request to those services, which may receive your IP address and browser information as a normal, unavoidable part of delivering the file — this is standard for any website using externally hosted fonts or icon sets, and is not used by us to track you individually.

The application form

Submitting the admission application form sends your entered information directly to Formspree, our form-processing provider, and does not set a cookie in your browser as part of that submission.

To the school/developer: If analytics (e.g. Google Analytics), a chat widget, social media embeds, or advertising pixels are added to this site in future, this policy must be updated, and a cookie consent banner must be added before any non-essential cookie is set, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Managing cookies in your browser

Even though this site does not set tracking cookies, you can still control cookies generally through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting cookies from any site you visit.
