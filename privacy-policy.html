2. Information we collect

We only collect the information needed to process an admission application:

  • Student's first and last name, date of birth, gender, and grade applied for
  • Parent/guardian's full name, relationship to the student, email address, phone number, and home address

We do not ask for information beyond what is needed to consider an application. We do not knowingly collect information about a child directly from the child; this form is intended to be completed by a parent or guardian.

3. How we use your information

  • To review and process admission applications
  • To contact the parent/guardian about the application and, where consent is given, about school updates and events
  • To keep basic administrative records if the student is admitted

4. Legal basis for processing

We process this information with your consent, given when you submit the application form and tick the consent box, and because processing is necessary to consider your child's admission.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. The application form is submitted through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service that delivers submissions to the School by email; Formspree may process and store submissions on servers outside Ghana as part of providing this service. Fonts and icons on this site are loaded from Google Fonts and Cloudflare's cdnjs, which may receive your device's IP address as a normal part of serving those files. See our Cookie Policy for more on this.

7. Your rights

Under Act 843, you have the right to know what personal data we hold about you or your child, to request a copy of it, to ask us to correct inaccurate data, to ask us to delete it, and to object to certain uses.
